# Phase 7 — Quality assurance

## Goal
Validate the storefront and document launch blockers.

## Checklist
- [ ] Responsive checks at narrow mobile, tablet, laptop, and wide desktop.
- [ ] Keyboard-only navigation and visible focus.
- [ ] Screen-reader labels and semantic heading structure.
- [ ] Color contrast and reduced-motion checks.
- [ ] SEO metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap/robots behavior.
- [ ] Performance and image optimization.
- [ ] Dependency, secret, and security review.
- [ ] Content/source verification.
- [ ] Jurisdiction, payment, fulfilment, privacy, terms, and consumer-law review by qualified parties.
- [ ] Confirm commerce remains disabled unless all approvals are documented.

## Acceptance criteria
- QA report lists pass/fail and evidence.
- All critical defects are fixed or explicitly accepted by the owner.
- Launch blockers are clearly identified; no legal clearance is inferred by technical QA.
