# Product data guide

Only publish product facts supported by reliable, traceable source material.

## Suggested fields
- `id`, `slug`, `name`, `category`
- `description` (reviewed, non-therapeutic)
- `identity` and `specifications` (each with source and verification state)
- `packaging` (only if confirmed)
- `documentation` (document type, batch association, issue date, verification state)
- `availabilityStatus`
- `jurisdictionReferences`

## Rules
- Do not invent product ratios, amount per vial, purity, sequence, supplier, origin, manufacturing standard, sterility, or storage claims.
- Do not imply that a sample COA is a real batch certificate.
- Mark demo products as illustrative and non-purchasable.
- Avoid dosing, reconstitution, injection, treatment, and human-use guidance.
- Research articles need authorship, source links, publication date, and review date.
