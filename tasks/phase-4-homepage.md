# Phase 4 — Homepage

## Goal
Build and review the storefront homepage only.

## Suggested content
- Clear brand proposition and primary shop/library navigation.
- Featured categories or products (demo data visibly marked).
- Quality/documentation explanation without unsupported claims.
- Editorial research entry points.
- Clear contact/footer information.

## Acceptance criteria
- Responsive, accessible, and performant.
- No fake testimonials, invented metrics, or unsupported claims.
- Product links lead to real catalogue routes or clearly marked placeholders.
- No checkout or payment actions.
