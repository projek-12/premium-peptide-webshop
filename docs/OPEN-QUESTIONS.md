# Open questions

These questions must be answered with evidence or explicit owner decisions. Do not silently guess.

1. **Brand:** What is the final brand name and domain? Until then, use a neutral placeholder and keep it easy to replace.
2. **Business model:** Which operating model is intended? This affects fulfilment, customer terms, payment eligibility, and market access.
3. **Product classification:** What is the documented classification of each product in each intended destination market?
4. **Target markets:** Which countries are in the first research scope? No country is enabled by default.
5. **Legal review:** Who will provide qualified market-specific review, and where will decisions be recorded?
6. **Product documentation:** Will authentic, batch-linked COAs or other technical documents be supplied? Who verifies them?
7. **Photography:** Are there owned product photographs, licensed supplier assets, or a planned photoshoot?
8. **Catalogue scope:** Which products and categories are genuinely intended for the initial catalogue?
9. **Languages:** Is English the initial storefront language, with localization later?
10. **Hosting and operations:** Who will own deployment, domain, analytics, privacy tooling, and ongoing maintenance?
